About Panchtatva

Named for the five elements — earth, water, fire, air, and space — Panchtatva is the Environmental Studies Society of Zakir Husain Delhi College (Evening), University of Delhi.

Who We Are

A student-run environmental society, active since at least 2025

Panchtatva organizes environmental awareness campaigns, plantation drives, workshops, field visits, and flagship fests across the academic session — led entirely by a student Core Team under faculty guidance. Dr. Smita Sundaram serves as Convenor, guiding the society's direction.

Mission & Vision

Preserving Nature. Inspiring Action. Building Tomorrow.

The society's activities consistently point to a mission of building environmental awareness on campus and beyond — through hands-on drives (plantation, cleanup), public education (workshops, webinars, campaigns), and youth leadership development (a full Core Team structure that rotates and mentors new leaders each session).

Values

🌱 Real Action

Plantation and cleanup drives with concrete, documented outcomes — not just messaging.

🤝 Collaboration

22 external partnerships in a single session, from foundations to fellow college societies.

🎓 Youth Leadership

A full student-run Core Team structure, handed down and rebuilt each session.

📣 Public Voice

A consistently active public presence — 349 posts and 86 reels in one session.
